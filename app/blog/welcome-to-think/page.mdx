## Introduction

We're excited to launch THINK, a new platform dedicated to teaching with and about AI in humanities classes. Funded by the National Endowment for the Humanities and based at UC Santa Cruz, THINK serves as a clearing house for AI-enabled historical simulations, pedagogy materials, and educational resources.

## Our Vision

The rapid advancement of AI technologies presents both opportunities and challenges for humanities education. Large language models like Claude can help create immersive historical simulations, generate thoughtful analysis, and assist with research—but they also raise important questions about authenticity, critical thinking, and the nature of historical knowledge itself.

THINK is built on the belief that humanities educators and students should actively engage with these technologies, learning to use them creatively while maintaining a critical perspective on their capabilities and limitations.

## What We Offer

### Historical Simulations

We've developed several AI-powered historical simulations, including:

- **Young Darwin**: An interactive simulation of Charles Darwin's formative years and voyage on the Beagle
- **History Simulator**: A tool for creating and exploring historically plausible scenarios
- **Apothecary Simulator**: An immersive experience in historical medicine and pharmacy

Each simulation is designed to be both engaging and pedagogically valuable, encouraging students to think critically about historical evidence, context, and interpretation.

### Pedagogy Materials

We provide freely available teaching materials including:

- Sample syllabi for courses incorporating AI tools
- Assignments that promote critical engagement with AI-generated content
- Guides for building your own historical simulations
- Best practices for responsible AI use in the classroom

### Community Resources

THINK also curates links to tools, platforms, and resources from the wider digital humanities community, helping educators discover and share effective approaches to AI-enabled teaching.

## The Road Ahead

This is just the beginning. We'll be regularly adding new projects, pedagogy materials, and resources. We're also eager to feature work from the community—if you've created AI-enabled humanities projects or teaching materials, we'd love to hear from you.

We invite you to explore the site, try out our simulations, and join us in thinking critically and creatively about the role of AI in humanities education.

## Get Involved

Whether you're an educator looking for new teaching tools, a student interested in AI and history, or a developer wanting to build humanities applications, there's a place for you in the THINK community.

Explore our projects, check out the pedagogy materials, and don't hesitate to reach out with questions, suggestions, or contributions.
